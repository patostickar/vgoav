Scene Blueprint

A playable Crash-Warped-style hub: one circular stone room, four portal levels selected by physically driving onto pressure pads.

Game states

  • gameState = 'MENU' — this Warp Room.
  • gameState = 'ARGENTINA' — Portal 1's endless runner. Reach 500 points to clear it; a TNT hit sends you home.
  • Cleared portals get a glowing green checkmark; each clear unlocks the next portal.

Room architecture

  • Circular arena, R = 16u wall, H = 11u, 12 chunky pilasters + capstones.
  • Tiered central dais (R 3.2u) with a glowing core — also a soft collision island.
  • Low-poly flat-shaded geometry, saturated stone palette, fog + starfield for warped-space sky.

Portals & pads (4× at corners)

  • Portal arches set into the wall at 45° / 315° / 135° / 225°, facing the center.
  • Glowing pressure pad R 2u on the floor at radius 9.2u in front of each portal; chevrons point inward.
  • Drive onto a pad → it depresses + lights up, portal flares, banner shows the level.

Asset blocking — Sardegna

  • Portal opening barricaded by 3 wooden planks + a diagonal brace (iron nails).
  • Two crossed iron chains meeting at a giant stylized iron padlock (body, shackle, keyhole, rivets).
  • Clearing the other 3 regions triggers the unlock: boards + lock break away and fall.

Camera rig (third-person)

  • Chase cam: 11u behind + 6.8u up, position & target lerp-smoothed each frame.
  • FOV 55°, looks at the rider + 1.8u. Press V for a top-down architectural view.
